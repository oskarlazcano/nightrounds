About

The IT partner that understands 24/7 care.

EO

Elijah Owens

Founder

Calm, competent, and on the clock when you are.

NightRounds is a remote-first IT, security, and compliance firm. Long-term care is our focus; small healthcare practices and businesses running Microsoft 365 are welcome too.

Elijah Owens founded NightRounds after working in healthcare-regulated environments, including Medicaid program administration and clinical research IT. That work is mostly about the unglamorous parts: who has access to what, whether it’s documented, and what happens when something breaks at the wrong time.

The goal is simple: be the IT partner a facility can actually reach, that writes things down, and that tells you plainly what is and isn’t covered.

How we work

The remote coverage model

Remote-first

Support is delivered remotely. Before service starts, we agree what can be handled remotely and who covers physical work at each site.

Built around your overnight

Elijah Owens’s workday falls during the US overnight shift. That’s why after-hours support is a core product here, not an expensive add-on.

Disclosed and authorized

Where the work is done from, which tools are used, and who has access are explained during qualification and written into your service agreement, before anyone touches your systems.
