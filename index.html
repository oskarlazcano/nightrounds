Can you work with our existing IT provider?

Yes. The co-managed model adds overnight support alongside your current provider, with written responsibilities, approved access, and a clear daytime handoff.

Is support remote?

NightRounds is remote-first. We agree what can be supported remotely and who handles physical work at each site before service begins.

Does this include every night and weekend?

Coverage is defined in your service plan. The website shows the available windows and any holiday or weekend exclusions before you buy.

Will you guarantee HIPAA compliance?

No. We help identify risks, coordinate improvements, and organize evidence within the agreed scope. Your organization keeps its compliance responsibilities.
