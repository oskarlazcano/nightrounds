How overnight coverage works

IT support built for your overnight shift.

NightRounds is set up around the hours your facility actually runs, so overnight help is designed in from the start, not bolted on.

Overnight support is included in the applicable plan during its published coverage window. Scope and response targets are agreed before onboarding.

Coverage window: published here before launch.

  1. Staff reach a person

    Through the agreed contact route for your plan. No phone tree, no voicemail-only queue.

  2. We verify who’s asking

    Identity checks before any reset or access change, so a call can’t be used to take over an account.

  3. Fix it or contain it

    Approved access fixes happen right away. Everything is logged in the ticket history.

  4. Morning handoff

    Anything left open goes to your administrator and daytime IT with an owner and a next step.

What’s included

  • Remote triage for supported users and devices
  • Identity verification before resets and access changes
  • Approved access fixes: passwords, MFA, locked accounts, workstation sign-in
  • Microsoft 365 and approved application-access help
  • Full ticket history you can review
  • Escalation and a written handoff to your daytime team

Where it stops

Written boundaries now mean no surprises later.

  • Covered hours only, as published in your plan
  • EHR access troubleshooting is not clinical workflow advice or an EHR vendor uptime guarantee
  • On-site dispatch requires a separate arrangement
  • Incident forensics requires a separate arrangement
  • Patient-safety events follow your facility’s emergency and downtime procedures
