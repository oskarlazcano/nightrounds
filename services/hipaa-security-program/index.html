How the program runs

A program, not a binder on a shelf.

  1. 1

    Understand the gaps

    Agreed assessment work across the systems in scope, with a risk register you own.

  2. 2

    Assign the fixes

    Each finding gets a remediation owner and a date. We coordinate; your leadership decides.

  3. 3

    Organize the evidence

    Policies, decisions, and proof of work kept in one place for surveys, insurers, and auditors.

  4. 4

    Review on a schedule

    Periodic reviews keep the program current as staff, systems, and vendors change.
