## Writing a post

1. Add a new `.md` file in `src/content/blog/`.
2. Fill in the title, description, date, and tags at the top.
3. Open a pull request. The preview link shows exactly how it will look.

## What a good morning handoff covers

- What happened overnight, in one line per issue
- What is still open, with an owner and next step
- Anything the day team or a vendor needs to act on
